Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

VidLoad is a browser extension that detects and downloads videos from webpages you visit. This document describes exactly what data the extension handles and what it does not.

What VidLoad does not collect

  • VidLoad does not send your browsing history to any server.
  • VidLoad does not send the URLs of videos you detect or download to any server.
  • VidLoad does not send your renamed labels, dismissed items, or settings to any server.
  • VidLoad does not use analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • VidLoad does not sell, share, or transfer user data.

What VidLoad processes locally

Data Purpose Stored where
Detected video URLs on the active tab Display downloadable videos in the popup. chrome.storage.session, cleared when the tab closes or browser restarts.
Titles, dimensions, poster images, and duration when available Help users identify videos. chrome.storage.session.
Renamed labels and dismissed items Remember popup preferences. chrome.storage.local, until uninstall or manual clearing.
Daily download count, if monetization is active Enforce the free-tier daily limit. chrome.storage.local.
Install timestamp, if monetization is active Track the free-trial period. chrome.storage.local.

Network requests VidLoad makes

  1. Downloading the video you asked for. For HLS or DASH streams, this includes fetching the stream segments.
  2. Reading manifest files for metadata such as duration and available quality options.
  3. Verifying payment status if monetization is active and you choose to upgrade.

VidLoad does not operate a backend and does not send browsing or download activity to a VidLoad server.

Third-party services

If monetization is active, VidLoad uses ExtensionPay and Stripe for payment processing. ExtensionPay and Stripe do not receive information about which videos you download or which sites you visit.

ExtensionPay privacy policy: https://extensionpay.com/privacy
Stripe privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy

Permissions

  • downloads: save the selected video file.
  • storage: remember renamed labels, dismissed items, trial timer, and daily count.
  • webRequest: detect video streams loaded by webpages by observing response headers.
  • offscreen: keep long-running stream-download jobs alive past service worker idle timeouts.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: rewrite Referer headers for extension-originated CDN requests when needed.
  • <all_urls>: detect videos on any site the user visits.

Data retention

Local data remains until you uninstall VidLoad or manually clear extension storage. There is no remote retention because VidLoad does not store this data remotely.

Children

VidLoad is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. VidLoad does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

To remove all local VidLoad data, uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions/. If you purchased Pro and want a refund or deletion of payment-account information, contact the support address below.
