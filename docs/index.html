Save videos from webpages.

VidLoad is a browser extension that detects videos on pages you visit and lets you save them to your computer.

What VidLoad does

VidLoad detects direct video files, HLS streams, and DASH streams, then presents them in a clean popup with quality selection, smart filenames, and one-click saving.

Detects common formats

MP4, WebM, HLS, DASH, and AES-128 encrypted HLS.

Runs locally

No browsing history, video URLs, or download activity are sent to a VidLoad server.

Built for clarity

Filters noisy stream segments, shows useful metadata, and lets you rename items before saving.
